About The Housefly

The Housefly is a unique first-person simulation game that puts you in the compound eyes of a common housefly. Unlike generic adventure games, our focus is on realistic insect behavior and environmental detail—every room, object, and threat is designed to mimic real-life housefly experiences.

Navigate through domestic environments like kitchens, living rooms, and gardens. Avoid common threats such as fly swatters, spiders, and bug sprays while completing challenges like finding food sources (fruit, garbage) or escaping closed windows.

With 5+ unique levels and atmospheric sound design, The Housefly offers a one-of-a-kind gaming experience for casual players and simulation fans alike. No downloads required—play directly in your browser for free!

Key Game Features

  • Immersive 3D graphics that capture the tiny world from a fly's perspective
  • Authentic housefly movements—glide, hover, and land on any surface
  • Challenging levels with increasing difficulty and hidden secrets
  • Atmospheric sound design (headphones recommended for full immersion)
  • Free to play—no registration or downloads needed
