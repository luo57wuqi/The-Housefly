About The Housefly

The Housefly is a unique first-person perspective game that puts you in the compound eyes of a common housefly. Navigate through various domestic environments, avoid threats, and complete insect-sized challenges.

Game Features

  • Immersive 3D environments from an insect's perspective
  • Realistic housefly movements and capabilities
  • Multiple levels with increasing difficulty
  • Unique challenges and obstacles to overcome
  • Stunning graphics and atmospheric sound design
