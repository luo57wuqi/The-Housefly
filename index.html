About The Housefly

The Housefly is a unique first-person game that puts you in the compound eyes of a housefly. Navigate domestic environments, avoid threats, and complete insect-sized challenges.

Features

  • Immersive 3D environments from insect perspective
  • Realistic housefly movements
  • Multiple levels with increasing difficulty
